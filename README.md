## Conviction AI · From market signal to evidence-backed investment decision

82.7% of US venture deal value now goes to AI companies, and 57% of investors spend 21+ hours a week on deal research. Faster summaries aren't the fix: a deck that claims 124% NRR produces a confident memo that says 124%. **The expensive mistakes come from numbers nobody checked.**

**Conviction AI is an investment-intelligence workspace for VC and PE teams that makes the evidence visible and the gaps impossible to miss.**

- 📡 **Discover:** an Opportunity Radar ranks AI sub-segments on an inflection index, with or without the mega-rounds
- 🎯 **Qualify:** fund thesis as editable rules, a deal pipeline with every stage change audited, and a Company 360 evidence board
- 🔬 **The signature catch:** the deck claims 124% NRR; recomputed from 16 customer rows it's 113%. Both values stay side by side until a reviewer resolves them
- 🧮 **The model never does the math:** NRR, runway, TAM, MOIC, IRR and LBO returns come from tested, deterministic engines, not the AI
- ⚠️ **Unknown ≠ pass:** anything not public shows as *Missing*, never guessed or scored by default
- 🏛️ **Decide:** underwriting, a bear case, a rule-based recommendation and an IC memo draft, held back from IC until conflicts are resolved
- 🏗️ **Value creation for PE:** LBO-lite, a buy-and-build Add-on Screener, Exit Readiness and AI growth options for portfolio companies
- 🌐 **Real companies, real sources:** live research builds company packs where every fact links to its page eg:ParentSquare
- 🗂️ **CRM-shaped from day one:** Postgres modelled on Salesforce objects, so a firm on Salesforce could sync it field for field

**Stack:** PostgreSQL · FastAPI · React (one self-contained HTML file) · Claude research agent with web search · open the HTML file to try it, no install needed

 ## GTM Orbit - Every account has a next move. Orbit helps you find it.

Sellers spend only about 40% of their time selling, and 69% of B2B buyers double-check AI-generated insights with a rep. More AI isn't the fix. AI that sellers can trust is.

**GTM Orbit turns a ranked account into a cited, validated outreach brief in under a minute, and the seller stays in control.**

- 🔍 **Discover:** finds the buying group, flags missing roles, and never uses opted-out contacts
- 📚 **Research:** pulls only facts with a source and a date from Salesforce, gong call(transcripts,trackers,insights etc) and product signals
- ✍️ **Compose:** writes the first email, mapping every claim to its evidence
- 🛡️ **Validate:** rejects made-up claims ("Series C", "three new regions") and sends the draft back to be rewritten
- ✅ **Human approval:** only the account owner can approve, and nothing is ever sent automatically
- ⚠️ **Unknown ≠ low:** stale or missing scores are flagged, never hidden as "low priority"
- 🧾 **Full audit trail:** every agent step and human decision is logged in Snowflake
- 💬 **Where sellers work:** the same flow runs in a web console and in Slack (`/orbit brief northstar`)
- 🔐 **Governed by design:** agents read one governed view; the app logs in as a key-pair service user with least-privilege access

**Stack:** Salesforce · Snowflake (agents run in the warehouse) · Slack Bot · Node.js · runs offline in 2 minutes, no accounts needed

## Readaroo - Never wait for the next book, and never read alone.

Readaroo is a family library app prototype. When a book your kid wants is on hold, it finds similar books you can borrow from your libraries tonight. It keeps one holds list across every library card in the family, lets parents set age bands and approve exceptions, and sends a weekly family reading digest. Libraries can fund instant borrows for series continuations, metered through Stripe in test mode, with a hard daily and monthly cap that is never exceeded.
