Conviction AI is an investment-intelligence workspace for VC and PE teams. It takes a deal from “which market is inflecting?” to an IC memo, then on to buy-and-build add-ons, exit readiness and AI growth options for a portfolio company.

Every number is computed by tested code rather than the AI. Conflicting sources are kept side by side, and anything that isn’t public is shown as Missing instead of guessed. The demo story: a pitch deck claims 124% net revenue retention, and the customer data says 113%.

It’s built on Postgres (modelled on Salesforce objects), FastAPI and a one-file React UI, with real companies researched from public sources, including Serent-backed ParentSquare.

**GTM Orbit** turns a ranked sales account into an outreach brief a seller can trust. Four AI agents (Discover → Research → Compose → Validate) build the brief inside Snowflake from Salesforce, call and product data, and every claim must cite its source or the validator sends it back.
The seller reviews and approves in a web console or in Slack (`/orbit`), and nothing is ever sent automatically.
Built with Salesforce, Snowflake, Slack (Bolt) and Node.js. It runs offline in 2 minutes with no accounts.
